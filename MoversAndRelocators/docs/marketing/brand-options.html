Name and logo options

Four directions for the relocation operations platform. Each logo works on light and dark backgrounds and as a small app icon.

Movora Recommended

"Every move. One clear view."

From move + -ora (era, aura). Short, easy to say in English, Arabic and Hindi, and not tied to one country. The mark is a packing box whose lid becomes a forward arrow: goods on their way.

Shiftwise

"Move smarter, from survey to settled."

"Shifting" is how many customers in India and the Gulf say "moving house". Two homes, one moving forward, show a household changing address. A calm, trustworthy blue.

Relocore

"The core system for relocation companies."

Says what it is: the central system (the "core") behind a relocation business, including the accounting. A box-shaped hexagon carries a route from start to the destination pin. Suits a B2B, ERP-style pitch.

Crateway

"Pack it. Track it. Deliver it."

Packing crate + the way it travels. Warm, energetic and very easy to remember. A strong fit if you want to lead with packing, labels and box tracking.

Before you settle on a name, check it in your markets: company and trademark registries (India, UAE and others), app stores and the .com / .in / .ae domains. I haven't checked availability.
